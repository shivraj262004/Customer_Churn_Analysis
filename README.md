# Customer_Churn_Analysis

# Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn data to identify key factors contributing to customer retention and attrition. By leveraging Python for data preprocessing, visualization, and statistical analysis, actionable insights were derived to inform business strategies and improve customer loyalty.


# Objectives
Understand Customer Behavior: Analyze factors like gender, senior citizen status, and tenure to determine their impact on churn.
Explore Contract and Payment Trends: Assess how contract type and payment methods influence churn rates.
Service Analysis: Identify services that correlate with higher or lower churn rates, such as streaming options and online security.
Visualize Key Metrics: Provide clear, insightful visualizations to communicate findings effectively.


# Tools & Technologies
Programming Language: Python
Libraries:
pandas for data manipulation
numpy for numerical analysis
matplotlib and seaborn for visualizations


# Key Insights
Churn Rate: Approximately 26.54% of customers have churned.
Senior Citizen Trends: Senior citizens exhibit a higher churn rate (41.7%) compared to non-senior citizens (23.6%).
Contract Types: Customers with month-to-month contracts are more likely to churn than those with one-year or two-year contracts.
Payment Methods: Electronic check users show higher churn rates compared to other payment methods.
Service Impact:
Customers with fiber-optic internet and streaming services have higher churn rates.
Online security, backup, and tech support services correlate with lower churn rates.
